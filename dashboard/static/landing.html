How it works

Point it at a city. Get back verdicts, not listings.

STEP 1

It scans the whole market

Every for-sale listing in the city you name, not a sample. A full Los Angeles run covers hundreds of houses in one pass.

STEP 2

It underwrites every house

After-repair value from real sold comps. Rehab priced from age and condition signals. Financing, closing, holding, and selling costs, all itemized.

STEP 3

It calls the deal

STRONG FLIP, MARGINAL FLIP, or NO DEAL, each with a score, the full math behind it, and a printable memo you can hand to a partner or lender.
